Suggested thesis caption

Figure X. Multi-dimensional stakeholder assessment based on the qualitative interviews. The horizontal axis represents organisational decision-making power; the vertical axis represents accessibility knowledge and/or experiential expertise; bubble size represents mobilisation capacity. Colours indicate stakeholder categories. Scores from 1 to 5 are interpretive and are intended to compare relative positions rather than provide objective measurements. The comparatively empty high-power/lower-expertise quadrant also reflects the limited representation of senior organisational decision-makers in the interview sample.
